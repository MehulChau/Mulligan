Physics: point-mass model, air density 1.225 kg/m³, lift and drag coefficients from spin ratio, ~3%/s spin decay, from the shared @mulligan/physics module. Rollout depends on the surface the ball lands on (fairway, green, rough, sand) and isn't modeled here — that's the M2 shot resolver's job, using the landing speed and descent angle below. Calibrate against your real gapping numbers at the range.
